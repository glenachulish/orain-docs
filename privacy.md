# Privacy Policy — Òrain

*Last updated: 5 November 2026*

Òrain collects nothing.

That is the whole policy, but the App Store requires it spelled out, and a
one-line answer to a serious question deserves the detail behind it. Òrain runs
on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and everything below applies to all three.

## What the app stores, and where

Your songs, versions, chords, notes, ratings, collections and tags are kept in
a database inside the app's own container on your device. Recordings you make
and files you import — audio, video, PDFs — are kept as files in that same
container.

None of it is sent to the developer. There is no Òrain account, no Òrain server,
and no sign-in. Nobody, including the developer, can see your library.

## iCloud sync

If iCloud is available, Òrain keeps your library in step across your own
devices through your own private iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit), and your
recordings travel with it. That data lives in your iCloud, under your Apple
Account, and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot read
it. You can turn sync off in Settings, and with it off nothing leaves the
device.

If you have iCloud Backup switched on, your device may also include the app's
data in your own encrypted backup. That is Apple's backup of your device, under
your Apple Account.

## Buying the full version

Òrain is free to download, with room for 10 songs of your own. A one-time
in-app purchase, bought through the App Store, removes the limit. Apple handles
the payment and tells the app only whether the purchase has been made. The
developer does not receive your name, payment details or any other personal
information, and Òrain stores nothing about you for this.

## What the app does not do

- No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting of any kind.
- No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
- No tracking, and no sharing of anything with any other company.
- No accounts, no email address, no name, no location.
- No third-party SDKs.

## The microphone

If you record yourself singing, your device asks your permission for the microphone
the first time. The recording is written straight to a file in the app's own
container. It is not transmitted, not processed anywhere else, and not
transcribed. You can delete it from within the app, and deleting the app
removes everything.

You can withdraw the permission at any time in Settings, under Privacy &
Security → Microphone, or under Òrain's own entry in Settings.

## Links you paste in

You can attach a link to a song — a video on YouTube, a track on Apple Music,
a page anywhere on the web. When you tap play, that link is opened in a web
view, and at that moment your device contacts that company's servers exactly as
your browser would. What they collect is governed by their privacy policy, not
this one.

Two things worth knowing about that:

- Nothing identifying Òrain or you travels with it. The app adds no identifier
  and receives nothing back.
- It only happens when you tap. Nothing is contacted while you are reading the
  words, and a song with no links contacts nothing ever.

## Apple Music, Spotify and Bandcamp

From version 1.3, a song on Apple Music, Spotify or Bandcamp linked to a song
can play in a bar at the top of the song page.

- **Apple Music** plays through Apple's MusicKit. The first time, your device
  asks your permission for Òrain to use Apple Music. Apple checks your
  subscription and plays the song; Òrain receives the song and whether it may
  play it, and nothing else about you or your library. You can withdraw the
  permission in Settings (on a Mac, System Settings → Privacy & Security →
  Media & Apple Music). A practice loop you set on a song is kept with the
  link in your own library, like everything else, and nowhere else.
- **Spotify** and **Bandcamp** play in their own players, loaded from their
  servers when you tap play, exactly as a web page would. If you choose
  *Sign in to Spotify*, you sign in on Spotify's own page; Òrain never sees
  your email address or password, and the sign-in is kept by the device's web
  view, not by Òrain.
- For a Bandcamp link, Òrain fetches the Bandcamp page once to find its player,
  and remembers the player's number on the device.
- **Searching** for a recording: Apple Music is searched through MusicKit
  (Apple), which needs the same permission but not a subscription; ▶ plays
  Apple's 30-second preview from Apple's servers. A Spotify search opens
  DuckDuckGo's results limited to Spotify's pages, and a Bandcamp search opens
  Bandcamp's own search page, each in a web page inside the app. The words you
  search for go to that company, as they would in a browser.

What those companies collect is governed by their privacy policies.

## Files you share out

Sharing a song or a library uses the standard iOS share sheet, so the file goes
wherever you choose to send it and nowhere else.

## Children

Òrain has no accounts, no messaging and no user-generated content visible to
anyone else. The one in-app purchase is made through the App Store, and Apple
restricts it to the person who holds the Apple Account (or whoever Family
Sharing allows). Òrain collects nothing from anyone of any age.

## Changes

If this ever stops being true, the policy will be updated before the change
ships, and the date at the top will say when.
