# Òrain — Support

Òrain is a song library for singers: the words, the chords above them, and the
recordings and links that go with a song, on your iPhone, iPad and Mac, and off the internet.

## Getting help

Email **88_intake.troupes@icloud.com**. Say which device you are on and what
you were doing, and include a screenshot if there is anything to see.

## Common questions

**Is Òrain free?**
Òrain is free to download, and you can add up to 10 songs of your own at no
cost. The eight songs that come with the app do not count towards the 10. A
single one-time purchase then unlocks unlimited songs, for good, on iPhone,
iPad and Mac, and Family Sharing is on. It is not a subscription.

**Where are the eight songs that come with the app?**
If your library is empty, tap *Add the sample songs*. If you already have songs
of your own, they are in the **⋯** menu at the top of the song list: *Add the
sample songs*. Any you deleted can be brought back the same way, and any you
have edited are left as they are. (Up to version 1.2.1 they only appeared by
themselves when iCloud was off, so most people never saw them.)

**What happens when I reach 10 songs?**
Everything you have keeps working: reading, editing, recording, transposing and
exporting. Only adding a new song is paused — a new song, an import that would
take you over the limit, and *Make it its own song*. Unlock, and it carries on.

**I bought Òrain before it was free. Do I have to pay again?**
No. If you bought the paid version, everything is already unlocked. If it ever
asks, open Settings → Licence and tap *Restore Purchase*.

**How do I restore my purchase on a new device?**
Settings → Licence → *Restore Purchase*, signed in with the same Apple Account.
One purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac.

**Where are my songs kept?**
On your device, in the app's own storage. There is no Òrain account and no
Òrain server. If iCloud is on, your library is also kept in step across your own
devices through your own iCloud account, which the developer cannot read; you
can turn that off in Settings. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

**How do I share one song, or its words?**
Open the song and use the share button in the toolbar — *Share the words* sends
plain text anyone can read, *Share the song file* sends a file that opens in
Òrain at the other end. Both are also in the **⋯** menu.

**How do I get my songs onto another device?**
Export the library from the Songs tab — the download-arrow button, then *Export
this library…* — and send the file to the other device however you like. Open
it there and choose *Import a library…*. Recordings and imported files do not
travel in that file; links do.

**How do I add a song?**
The **+** button at the top of the song list. Give it a title, then type or
paste the words. Chords go in square brackets in front of the syllable they
land on: `[G]Ailein duinn`. If you paste words with chords written on the line
above, the editor offers to convert them.

**How do I change the key?**
Open a song, then the **⋯** menu → *Change key…*. Choose the chord you want to
start on and every other chord moves with it. It saves as a new version, so the
original is still there; versions can be renamed, deleted, or made the default.

**Can I record myself singing it?**
Yes — in the media box on the song page, *Add* → *Record*. The first time, iOS
will ask for the microphone. Recordings stay on the device.

**The words scroll off before I finish the verse.**
The scroll speed is the slider on the auto-scroll bar, and it remembers what
you set. Tap stop and it stays where it is; start again and it carries on from
there.

**Can I make the words bigger?**
Yes, with the size control on the song page. Òrain also follows the system text
size, so if you have made text larger across the phone, the words are already
larger here.

**A YouTube link says the video cannot be played.**
Some videos are set by whoever uploaded them to refuse being played inside
other apps. Nothing can be done about that from this end — the card offers to
open it in YouTube instead.

**Something is wrong with the Gaelic in the songs that came with the app.**
Please tell me. Those eight songs are traditional and out of copyright, added
so the app has something in it on the first day, and the spelling is worth
getting right.

## What Òrain does not do

No accounts, no subscription, no advertising, no analytics. The one purchase is a single payment, once. It works with the
phone in flight mode, which is the point — the places people sing are often the
places with no signal.
